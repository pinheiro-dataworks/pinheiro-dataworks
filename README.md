## Hello! I'm Renan Pinheiro 👋

## About Me
🚀 Data Scientist building production-oriented Machine Learning and AI systems that connect data, models, engineering, and business decisions.

My work spans predictive modeling, Generative AI, RAG, MLOps, large-scale data processing, fraud & anomaly detection, Graph ML, computer vision, and decision intelligence, with a strong focus on rigorous evaluation, reproducibility, explainability, monitoring, and production readiness.

I bring 11+ years of domain expertise in Construction and Real Estate, which I combine with Data Science to solve complex business problems in areas such as cost forecasting, risk intelligence, financial analytics, and operational decision-making. Beyond PropTech, my portfolio explores international use cases across fraud & financial risk, e-commerce, customer support AI, marketing intelligence, computer vision, and large-scale ML systems.

My projects are designed around the full ML lifecycle:

Business Problem → Data Engineering → Feature Engineering → Modeling → Evaluation → Deployment → Monitoring → Business Impact

Current focus: Production ML · Generative AI · RAG & Agents · Databricks · MLOps · Graph Machine Learning · Fraud & Risk Intelligence

Let's collaborate on something amazing!
